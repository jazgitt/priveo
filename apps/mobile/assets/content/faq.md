# Priveo help

## How does billing work?
The default plan billing cycle is 30 calendar days from the first service date set by Priveo. Pauses and exterior visits skipped by operations extend the renewal date. Prices include GST. You pay manually by UPI and tap “I've paid”; Priveo confirms receipt. Issued invoices appear in billing history.

## What happens if I pay late?
By default, a plan payment Over due for seven days makes the subscription Inactive. Contact Priveo to reactivate it. Payment alone does not restart service: Priveo sets the new first service date and activates a new cycle. An overdue add-on payment does not inactivate the subscription.

## How do I skip a visit?
Open Skip or pause from Home and choose the date. The default change deadline is 9 PM the previous evening; Priveo can configure it. After the deadline, call the helpline. Customer skips do not extend the renewal date.

## Can I pause my plan?
Choose a start and end date in Skip or pause. Paused days extend the plan, and ending a pause early recalculates the renewal date. A skip inside a pause is counted once. The configured previous-day deadline applies (9 PM by default).

## What is the interior care day?
When your plan includes interior cleaning, choose one day each week. A skipped or paused interior day is lost for that week and is not made up.

## How do I see proof of service?
Open Service Details from the calendar or service history. Completed work shows the crew's checklist, photos and videos. A visit is complete once all its jobs are done or skipped.

## How do I report a problem?
Open Report an issue, select the relevant visit when available, explain the problem and attach photos. My complaints shows progress and the latest notes. Use the helpline for urgent changes.

## How do I book Deep Spa?
For a vehicle with an Active subscription, open Add-ons, choose Deep Spa and propose a date and finish-by time. It is bought separately from the plan. Change or cancel by 9 PM the previous evening. For a vehicle without a subscription, Priveo creates your customer/vehicle details first. Then open Deep Spa from Home and use the same booking, payment and service tracking flow.
